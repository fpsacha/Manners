### In 1.11.41

- **Buttons for the moment** (In a fight > Buttons for the moment): data-driven
  buttons, armed before the pull, invisible until their moment, drawn as cards
  of the player's look above the prompt (Card size slider). Mage: Counterspell,
  Blink, Ice Block, Cold Snap, Evocation, mana gem, Slow Fall, Spellsteal, Fire
  Ward and Frost Ward; every mana class: Drink out of a fight; shaman, priest,
  hunter, warrior: Purge, Dispel Magic, Tranquilizing Shot, Shield Slam (off).
  Frost Nova left out at the owner's word. A cover swallows clicks while a
  card is dark; where only the game knows (hidden health, mana, the interrupt
  flag) a click casts on the second of two. Keys always cast at once.
  "Show where the buttons for the moment sit" previews them out of a fight.
  Urgent cards light at once, during the global cooldown, with no fade.
- **Wards by the enemy's cast school**: exact on Classic Era and TBC (a table
  from the client data, `CastSchools.lua`); on Forever the game is asked to
  light the ward from the hidden spell id, else the ward follows what has hit
  you this fight.
- **Cure in a fight**: Forever refuses addons the list of removable debuffs
  (a player proved it with a /run). The cure button now carries the game's own
  aura display (`Prompt/CureNeed.lua`), two clicks where only the game knows;
  Shift and Ctrl per cure (Plain press / With Shift / With Ctrl); one cure
  switch (Who to buff points to In a fight).
- **Report a bug** window (launcher, footer, /manners bug, a chat link on a new
  error): category, words, a GitHub link prefilled for the issue form, the full
  check-up to paste. **Mark this moment** (key, launcher, /manners mark). Fight
  summaries and hold-back lines on the trail; more self-test checks;
  `tools/read_report.py` triages a pasted check-up.
- **Water and food for your target** (mage, Who to buff, off by default; the
  owner tested it in game: it works), filling the trade window.
- **Fire Shield from the imp on the tank** (off by default; needs the tank role).
- Fixes: Fortitude's rank for low-level players (three causes), the thank-you
  lost after a pause, In character for classes with nothing to give (a hunter),
  Unending Breath only while swimming (switch), key boxes that take keyboard
  keys (a focused hidden text field), the r39 bug pass (20 fixes), the cast
  watch's counts, one trail line per loss of control, and molnare's PR 34
  (Righteous Fury for a solo Protection paladin, merged with authorship).

  22 GB), and scenario files left globals replaced for the next file (97 false

  table after each file; a leaked non-function global would still get through.
